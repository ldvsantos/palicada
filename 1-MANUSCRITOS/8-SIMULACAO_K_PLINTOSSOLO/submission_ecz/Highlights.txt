Highlights

- Five-parameter multiplicative correction integrates hydraulic, phytotoxic and geotechnical amplifiers of USLE K
- Sobol analysis shows hydraulic impedance governs 63-78% of amplification variance in Plinthosols
- Hydraulic-biological parameters recoverable with <15% bias under 30% noise
- Green-Ampt model validates infiltration-rate threshold separating Plinthosol from Ferralsol runoff regimes
- Geotechnical equifinality confined to shallow features is mitigable by slope-stability priors
